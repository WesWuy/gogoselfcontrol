About

What this is.

Help men who need their men to find their men. A directory of men's gatherings — circles, ministries, recovery rooms, mentorships, hiking collectives, therapeutic groups, civic and faith fraternals.

The directory

351 organizations across 138 jurisdiction files. We aggregate. We don't run them. We don't rank them. We tell you what's there. You decide where to walk in.

Who this is for

Priority: the man in crisis with nowhere obvious to go. Other users — women assessing safety, facilitators, funders, men already in the work — are real and welcome, but secondary.

Faith and tone

Religion-dry by default. Listed entries describe themselves accurately — a Christian organization is named as Christian, a Sufi order is named as Sufi — but the directory's own voice stays plain.

What this isn't

  • Not a vetting service. We don't assess organizations on safety, quality, or ethics.
  • Not a ranking. We don't say one is better than another.
  • Not a clinical service. We are not licensed to diagnose, treat, or counsel.
  • Not a substitute for a crisis line. If you are in crisis, see the disclaimer.
