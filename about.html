About the project

What this is, and what it isn't.

gogoselfcontrol.org is a directory hub for men's gatherings. The mission line — "help men who need their men to find their men" — describes the work in a single sentence.

The directory

The directory aggregates 351 organizations that already exist and want to be found, across 138 jurisdiction files (countries, states, provinces, regions). We don't run them. We don't rank them. We don't recommend one over another. We tell you what's there. You decide where to walk in.

Who this is for

The priority user is the man in crisis with nowhere obvious to go. Every product decision is meant to serve him first. Other users — women assessing safety, facilitators, funders, men already in the work — are real and welcome, but secondary.

Faith and tone

The site is religion-dry by default. A man arriving here should not feel he's walked into a Christian space, or any specific tradition's space. Listed entries describe themselves accurately — a Christian organization is named as Christian, a Sufi order is named as Sufi — but the directory's own voice stays plain and tradition-neutral. Faith specificity comes from each entry, not from the framing.

What the directory deliberately is not

  • Not a vetting service. We do not assess organizations on safety, quality, or ethics.
  • Not a ranking. We do not say one is better than another.
  • Not a referral. We do not steer. We list the doors; you walk through.
  • Not a clinical service. We are not licensed to diagnose, treat, or counsel.
  • Not a substitute for a crisis line. If you are in crisis, see the disclaimer page.

The team

A small team running this on nights and weekends. The founder is the public face but not the entire brand — think Flo from Progressive: visible, recognizable, but the brand exists independently. Wes is the technical implementer. Anthony contributes ideas. The work pace is deliberately patient.

How we got here

The directory began as a research file in Obsidian. Each entry has YAML frontmatter specifying its category, tradition, intake pathway, cost, and whether it can receive a man in active crisis. The file format is open and the export is reproducible. As the directory expands, the underlying schema is the durable artifact — what you see on this page is one rendering of it.

What's next

The next gating piece of work is verification: moving entries from cold-listed (researched but not personally confirmed) to member-confirmed (verified by phone or in person). Until verification is widespread, treat every entry as a starting point for your own due diligence.
