Read this before you use the directory

Disclaimer.

Plain language. We don't want anyone harmed by the way this site works.

Cold-listing notice

Every entry on this site is currently cold-listed. That means an organization has been identified from public sources and added to the directory, but its current status, current programs, current door, and current safety have not been personally verified by a member of our team. URLs may be stale. Phone numbers may have changed. Programs listed here may no longer run.

Treat every entry as a starting point for your own due diligence. Call. Email. Read the org's current website. Ask a member. Do not assume that listing here is endorsement, certification, or current truth.

No vetting, no ranking, no recommendation

The directory aggregates. It does not assess organizations on safety, quality, ethical record, leadership, finances, or doctrine. We do not have the standing to make those judgments and would not pretend to. Listing is presence-based: an organization exists, has a door, and the door is publicly findable.

If you are in crisis right now

This site is a directory, not a crisis line. If you are in immediate danger to yourself or another person, please contact a crisis service directly:

  • United States: 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988)
  • Canada: 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline (call or text 988)
  • UK: Samaritans 116 123 / CALM 0800 58 58 58
  • Ireland: Samaritans 116 123 / Pieta 1800 247 247
  • Australia: Lifeline 13 11 14 / MensLine 1300 78 99 78
  • New Zealand: Lifeline 0800 543 354 / 1737 (Need to Talk)

If you're outside these countries and need a crisis number, the directory's crisis-safe filter will surface organizations that explicitly receive men in active crisis without prior screening.

Sovereignty respect for Indigenous communities

Tribal-internal traditions — Native American, First Nations, Métis, Inuit, Aboriginal Australian, Māori, AmaXhosa initiation, Hausa-Fulani emirate councils, Igbo umunna, and many others mentioned in our jurisdiction files — are real and not directory-listable in the form public organizations are. Listed Indigenous-led entries on this site are community-controlled organizations with public intake. Tribal-internal practice belongs to the communities that hold it. Outsiders should respect that distinction.

Trauma-informed framing

We try to write entries plainly. Some entries describe organizations whose programs have historically retraumatized people — including men who were the harm-causers, men who were the harmed, and women and children adjacent to either. We don't always know which is which from the outside. If an entry's framing feels off to you, trust your own instinct first; the directory's listing does not override your reading of an organization.

Directory does not authenticate

We do not run identity checks, background checks, or any other authentication on listed organizations or their leaders. If an organization claims a credential, accreditation, registration, or status, you should verify that claim directly with the relevant authority, not with us.

Liability and safe harbor

Listing on this site is not endorsement, partnership, affiliation, or sponsorship. The site operators do not accept liability for harm arising from interactions with listed organizations. Walk through any door at your own discretion and risk.

Removing or correcting an entry

If you represent an organization listed here and want the entry removed, corrected, or updated, please reach out via the contact link on the About page. We will respond promptly and respect reasonable removal requests without dispute.

Privacy

This site is a static-page directory. We do not run analytics, advertising tracking, cookies, or user accounts. Filter and search work entirely in your browser. The site does not store anything about your visit.
